Supplier Due Diligence Report: Orion Retail Group

Orion Retail Group buys its delivery vans from Bluefin Motors. Bluefin Motors is a subsidiary of Calder Industries.

Calder Industries is chaired by Elena Varga. Elena Varga is also a director of Mistral Shipping.

Mistral Shipping is on the EU sanctions list.

Orion Retail Group signed its cloud hosting contract with Skyline Data Centres. Skyline Data Centres is based in Frankfurt.

The cloud hosting contract with Skyline Data Centres was approved by Priya Nair, the Chief Technology Officer of Orion Retail Group.

Skyline Data Centres shares its registered office with Mistral Shipping at 8 Dockside Lane.

Orion Retail Group outsources payroll to Pinewood Payroll Services. Pinewood Payroll Services is audited by Hartley and Co.
